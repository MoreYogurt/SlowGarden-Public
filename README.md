# SlowGarden

Offline coloring journal for iPhone and iPad.

Little illustrated storybooks you color at your own pace, plus a private Journal that stays on your device. No numbers, scores, or streaks. Blank space is allowed.

- **Website:** https://getslowgarden.com/
- **App Store:** https://apps.apple.com/app/id6823141814
- **Category:** Lifestyle
- **Platforms:** iPhone & iPad (iOS / iPadOS 17+)
- **Languages:** English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French

### What’s free

Four launch books (16 drawings) with the full coloring toolkit. Offline. No ads. No account. No subscription.

### Optional unlock

A one-time Developer Supporter purchase unlocks more books, future books, and alternate icons. Previous purchases remain valid. There is no recurring fee.

### Private Journal

Journal entries live on your device and are never attached when you share artwork.
